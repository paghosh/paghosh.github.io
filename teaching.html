2026 Merrick Foundation Teaching Award, University of Oklahoma. I also hold a Certificate in University Teaching from Syracuse University (2013).

My courses share three habits. Students work with real data sets from the first week. Every method is tied to a published paper that the class replicates or extends. And in the undergraduate courses, the class meetings are for solving problems together rather than for lecture, with the lecture material delivered beforehand as notes and short videos.

Course materials are distributed through Canvas. Current and prospective students are welcome to email me for a syllabus.
