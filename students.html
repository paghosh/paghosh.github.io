For prospective students

PhD applicants. I advise students working in labor economics, health economics, and applied econometrics. Admission is through the Department of Economics; I chaired the PhD admissions committee from 2020 to 2023 and remain a member. If you are already admitted and would like to discuss working together, email me with a short note about the questions that interest you.

Master's applicants. See the Online MA in Econometrics page.

Undergraduates at OU. I supervise honors theses and directed readings in labor, health, and applied econometrics. Students who have taken ECON 4233 or an equivalent regression course are best prepared.
