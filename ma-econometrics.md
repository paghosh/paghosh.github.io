## About the program

The Online MA in Econometrics admitted its first cohort in Spring 2025. It is a 30 credit hour, fully online degree that can be completed in 15 to 21 months, depending on full time or part time enrollment and on prerequisites. The curriculum covers applied econometrics, machine learning methods, and Python programming for data analytics, with projects built around real data.

The program is designed for working professionals and recent graduates who want to move into data intensive roles in industry, government, consulting, or research, and for students who plan to apply to doctoral programs in economics or a related field.

## Courses I teach in the program

- **ECON 5213, Advanced Econometrics.** Regression methods and their applications, taught in Python (Spring 2025 and 2026).
- **ECON 5083, Applied Labor Economics.** Wage determination, inequality, and policy evaluation, with Python study guides (Summer 2026).
